1. Who we are

Jewar Kaushal Abhiyan ("the campaign", "we") is a public, non-party initiative by Foremost Computers, Jewar, Uttar Pradesh, asking for a public skill centre for the youth of Jewar. This policy explains what we collect on this website and how we use it.

2. What we collect

  • When you register support: your name, mobile number, village/ward/locality, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and (only if you tick the box) your consent to receive campaign updates on WhatsApp.
  • When you comment: the name and village you choose to give (or none, if you post anonymously) and your comment text.
  • For spam and bot protection: a one-way scrambled code (hash) made from your network address. We do not store your actual IP address and cannot reverse the code.
  • On your device: your browser may remember which reaction buttons you already pressed. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To count genuine support for the demand and show the total publicly.
  • To make sure one mobile number is counted only once.
  • To send campaign updates, only if you agreed to receive them.
  • To review comments before they are published and to stop spam.

4. What is shown publicly

Only the total number of supporters and village-wise totals. A village name appears only after it has 10 or more supporters; smaller numbers are grouped as "others". Your name and mobile number are never shown. Approved comments are shown with the name and village you chose to give.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or give your personal data to anyone: no political party, candidate, public representative or company. We may share only aggregate numbers (for example, "4,200 supporters from 35 villages").

6. Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) on servers in Mumbai, India, with access restricted to the campaign administrator. We keep it only while the campaign is active and delete personal details within 6 months after the campaign ends.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent for updates, at any time. Write to us or message us on WhatsApp (see Contact) with your mobile number; we will act within 7 days.

8. Children

Support can be registered only by people aged 18 or older. Younger students may ask a parent or guardian to register support on their behalf.
